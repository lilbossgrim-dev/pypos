# Governance

PyPOS is maintained by its maintainers (listed in the repository settings once published).
Decisions are made by consensus in issues and pull requests; when consensus is not reached the
maintainers decide.

- **Contributors** propose changes through pull requests.
- **Maintainers** review and merge, cut releases, and enforce the
  [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).
- Breaking changes require a note in `CHANGELOG.md` and, after 1.0, a major version bump.
- New maintainers are invited by existing maintainers after sustained, high-quality contribution.
